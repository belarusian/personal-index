# QA-12: RobotsCache.put() violates max_entries invariant and evicts on overwrite

Status: IMPLEMENTED PR#1221@a175b2f (cycle 219)
Priority: medium
Component: personal_index/robots_cache.py
Filed: Cycle 171

## Summary

Two related defects in `RobotsCache.put()`:

1. **max_entries=0 invariant violation**: When `max_entries=0`, `put()` still
   inserts the entry (size becomes 1 > max_entries=0). The `_evict_oldest()`
   call is a no-op on an empty dict, and the unconditional insert proceeds.

2. **Overwrite at capacity evicts an unrelated entry**: When the cache is at
   capacity and `put()` is called with an already-cached domain, it evicts the
   oldest entry (which may be a *different* domain) before inserting the update.
   The caller intended to update an existing entry, not to discard another.

Additionally, the class docstring claims "Thread-safe cache for robots.txt
results" but no `threading.Lock` exists and `put()` performs an unsynchronized
check-then-act (`if len >= max: evict; insert`).
